You want this answer to sound **mature and forward-looking**, not like you’re complaining about your current company.

Here’s a **strong, Hiring Manager–friendly version** 👇

---

I’ve had a great learning experience at my current company, especially working in a fast-paced startup where I got the opportunity to build products from scratch, take end-to-end ownership, and grow into a leadership role.

That said, I’m now looking for a few things that I believe will help me grow further. One is working at a larger scale — both in terms of users and system complexity — where I can contribute to more challenging engineering problems.

I’m also looking for better alignment with market compensation. In a startup environment, compensation is often tied to funding cycles, and since we haven’t raised our next round yet, the growth in compensation hasn’t kept pace with market standards.

Additionally, I’m planning a change in location from Gurgaon, so I’m exploring opportunities that align better with that.

Overall, I’m looking for a role where I can combine my experience with frontend architecture and performance with larger scale challenges and continue growing both technically and in terms of impact.

---

# 🎯 Why this works

* **Positive start** → no negativity
* **Growth-focused** → scale, impact
* **Compensation mentioned professionally**
* **Location reason included cleanly**
* **Clear intent**

---

# 🔥 If they probe deeper

### ❓ “Is compensation your main reason?”

Say:

> “It’s one of the factors, but not the only one. The bigger driver for me is working on larger-scale systems and continuing to grow technically.”

---

### ❓ “Why larger scale?”

> “Most of my experience has been in a startup environment where I built systems from scratch. Now I want to see how systems evolve and operate at scale with millions of users.”

---

# 🚀 Pro Tip

👉 Never say:

* “I’m underpaid”
* “Company is not good”

👉 Always frame as:
**“I’m looking for better alignment and growth”**
